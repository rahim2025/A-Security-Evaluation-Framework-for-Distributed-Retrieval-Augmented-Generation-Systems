What was fixed this session

  • DDoS — exact_match bug: a leaked chat-template role token ("system\nyes" instead of "yes") deflated every text-overlap metric, not just exact_match. Fixed in open_model.py, confirmed live. Details on the DDoS report.
  • KB extraction — rate-limit confound: a defense re-run silently returned docs_extracted=0 for both conditions because of a shared 60/min-per-IP budget exhausted by a preceding DDoS test, not because the attack/defense actually did nothing. probe_source() now tracks and surfaces this explicitly. Details on the KB report.
  • KB extraction — 82.5% block-rate caveat: the headline defense number was measured on a 10-topic corpus with a threshold set at half that count — real, but not yet representative at production topic scale. See the KB defense section.
